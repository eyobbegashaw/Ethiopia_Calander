About ባሕር ሀሳብ & ስንክሳር

The Ethiopian calendar is based on the ancient Coptic calendar and is approximately 7-8 years behind the Gregorian calendar. It has 13 months, with 12 months of 30 days and a 13th month of 5 or 6 days.

ስንክሳር (Synaxarium) contains the commemoration of saints for each day of the year according to the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.
