## 🎯 Key Features in Detail

### 📅 Ethiopian Calendar System

The Ethiopian calendar is based on the ancient Coptic calendar and is approximately 7-8 years behind the Gregorian calendar. It features:
- **13 months**: 12 months of 30 days + Pagume (5 or 6 days)
- **Leap years**: Every 4th year (Ethiopian year ending with 3)
- **Ethiopian numerals**: ፩, ፪, ፫, etc. for numbers
- **Amharic month names**: መስከረም, ጥቅምት, etc.

### ⛪ Synaxarium (ስንክሳር)

Daily saints and commemorations including:
- Saint biographies in Amharic and English
- Feast days and traditions
- Miracles and iconography
- Search by date or saint name

### 📖 Daily Readings (ግጻዊ)

- Old and New Testament readings
- Amharic translations
- Bookmark favorite readings
- Add personal notes
- Audio playback support

### 🎮 Quiz System

- **15 questions** per quiz generated by Google Gemini AI
- Categories: Synaxarium, Gizae, Holidays
- Difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard
- Score tracking and history
- Share quizzes in chat

### 💬 Community Chat

- **Public/Private groups** with customizable settings
- **Real-time messaging** with polling updates
- **File sharing** (images, documents)
- **Message reactions** (emojis)
- **Polls** with multiple voting options
- **Member management** (admins, moderators)
